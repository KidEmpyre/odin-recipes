Juicy Lucy Burgers

Description

This recipe is a delicious burger that can be made at home. They are packed with flavor using a number of ingredients.

Worcestershire sauce, black pepper, and garlic combine with the cheese filled center to create a delicious masterpiece.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 pounds ground beef
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 3/4 tsp garlic salt
  • 4 slices American cheese
  • 4 hamburger buns, split

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients
  2. Mix ground beef, Worcestershire sauce, pepper, and garlic together in a bowl until well combined
  3. Form into 8 thin patties, each slightly larger than a cheese slice.
  4. Cut each slice of American cheese into 4 equal pieces; stack the pieces
  5. Sandwich 1 stack of cheese between 2 ground beef patties.
  6. Tightly pinch the edges together to seal around the cheese. Be sure to seal tightly, or the cheese will burst through when cooked. Repeat with the remaining cheese and patties.
  7. Heat a large cast-iron skillet over medium heat. Cook patties in the hot skillet until well browned, about 4 minutes; they will puff due to steam from the melting cheese.
  8. Flip patties, prick the tops to release steam, and cook until bowned on the other side and no longer pink in the center; about 4 more minutes. An instant-read thermometer inserted into the center should read at least 160 degrees F (70 degrees C).
  9. Serve on hamburger buns.
